# Licensing & Provenance Policy

Agent Foundry does not silently assume that discovered code, Skills, assets, or documentation may be copied.

## Reuse rules

Before adopting or merging third-party material:
- identify the source and revision;
- identify the applicable license or explicit authorization;
- preserve notices/attribution when required;
- distinguish ideas/protocol compatibility from copying implementation;
- do not remove upstream provenance to make an integration look native;
- keep incompatible licensing as a real blocker until a lawful route exists.

## Skills

Published Skills should use the Agent Skills <code>license</code> field when applicable and include complete bundled terms when a short identifier is insufficient.

## Repository license

The overall Agent Foundry repository license is an owner decision and is **not guessed by automation**. Until the owner selects one, absence of a repository-wide license must not be interpreted as permission to reuse the whole repository under an assumed open-source license.

This does not override licenses already attached to individual upstream-derived components.
