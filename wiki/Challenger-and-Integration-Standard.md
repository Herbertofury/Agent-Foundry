# Challenger & Integration Standard

Before substantial invention, ask:

> **Who already solved part of this better, and how can we legally and technically integrate the strongest pieces?**

## Baseline to beat
The point is not merely to avoid reinventing the wheel. Identify the strongest credible current solution and treat it as a benchmark to **meet or beat**. Reuse/compose its superior authorized pieces where appropriate, preserve protected dimensions, and require evidence before claiming the final system is better. Parity is an intermediate result, not an automatic “best” claim.

## Search
Look across:
- upstreams;
- GitHub;
- GitLab;
- Codeberg;
- significant forks;
- package/plugin ecosystems;
- standards/reference implementations;
- authorized internal/commercial implementations.

## Decide
Use one explicit state:

<code>adopt</code> · <code>merge</code> · <code>port</code> · <code>wrap</code> · <code>backport</code> · <code>compose</code> · <code>reject</code> · <code>unresolved</code>

## Best-of-breed composition
One challenger does not need to win everything.

A stronger final system may combine the best parser, cache, compatibility layer, UI behavior, and local orchestration from different sources.

## Avoid the opposite failure
Research is not allowed to become the stall. Once the likely best candidates are understood and the integration set is actionable, implement.
